Treatments

What can be treated

Three lines of work that often combine in a single visit. There is no price list pasted up here: each session is agreed on what you need and how long it will take, and you hear that before anything begins.

01

Massage

The heart of the studio, and the reason most people come here for the first time.

  • Deep tissue massage

    Firm, sustained pressure on muscles that have been pulling for weeks. It is the treatment that comes up most in the reviews: people who arrive with a locked neck and leave without the knot.

  • Knots and tension

    For specific points — the neck, the shoulder blade, the lower back — that stretching at home does not shift. The area is worked along with what surrounds it, so the knot does not come straight back.

  • Full body massage

    A complete session when what you need is to properly switch off. It is also the one most often given as a present, and the studio keeps gift cards.

02

Advanced aesthetics

Devices and treatment skincare, always after the skin has been assessed first.

  • Laser hair removal

    One of the services the studio is recommended for most: clients who had tried other places and who noticed the difference here after years of sessions elsewhere.

  • Facial care

    Cleansing, hydration and treatment based on how your skin is that day, not on a fixed protocol. You leave with calmer skin and with instructions for home.

  • Targeted treatments

    One-off advanced aesthetics work decided in the room, once it is clear what the skin needs and what it does not.

03

Body and products

What you can take home, and the body work that goes beyond massage.

  • Body scrub

    Skin that needs renewing, especially before summer or after a long winter. It shows immediately and prepares the skin for the rest of the treatment.

  • Studio skincare

    Aloe gel, cream deodorant and a yoghurt scrub: products she uses and recommends because she has tried them, not because there is something to sell.

  • Gift cards

    For giving a session without having to choose the treatment yourself: whoever receives it decides what they want when they call. It is one of the details the reviews mention most.

If you are not sure which treatment is yours, say so in the message: four lines about what is going on is enough to point you in the right direction.

First visit

How the first time works

You do not need to know the name of the treatment or how many sessions you need. That gets decided there, talking.

The studio is small and visits are one at a time: there is no full waiting room, and nobody hurrying you because the next session is due. If you are coming about a specific problem, say how long you have had it and what you have already tried; if you are coming for aesthetics, say what has worked and what has not elsewhere.

Message on WhatsApp
  1. 1

    You send a message

    On WhatsApp, with what is going on and when suits you. No form to fill in and no call first.

  2. 2

    She replies with a proposal

    She tells you which treatment fits, how long it needs and what it will cost, before anything is booked.

  3. 3

    You come with time to spare

    Arrive a few minutes early so there is room to talk. If you have medical reports or creams you use, bring them.

  4. 4

    You leave with a plan

    After the session you know how you are, what to do at home, and — if you need to come back — when it makes sense to.

The studio

One professional, one room, and a lot of people who come back

Judit Quiromasaje is a massage and advanced aesthetics studio on Rambla de l'Exposició. There is a single practitioner behind it, and that explains how everything works.

People describe it the same way every time: she listens first, then works. The session does not follow a protocol but follows how you arrive that day, which is why the same hands suit a locked neck and skin that needs a specific treatment.

The reputation comes from word of mouth. More than a hundred and sixty reviews and an average of five out of five are not built with advertising: they happen when people who leave feeling better tell the people around them.
